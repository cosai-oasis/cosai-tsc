* **RSAC 2027 Proposal Submission and Deadlines:**   
  * J.R. and Jodi discussed the upcoming RSA 2027 call for presentations, which closes on October 9th, reminding participants to submit proposals via the tracking spreadsheet. Shrey inquired about the types of proposals to submit. J.R. suggested focusing on topical interest clusters such as agent sandboxing, security risk governance, CoSAI-RM, and Code Guard.  
* **TSC Weekly Meeting Times and Polling Results:**   
  * Claudia presented the results of the TSC meeting time poll. She reported that the 12:00 PM Eastern time slot received the most votes (16 votes), while a 7:00 PM slot was favored to accommodate the Asia-Pacific region. J.R. proposed—and the group agreed—to hold meetings at 12:00 PM Eastern for three out of four weeks in a cycle, and at 7:00 PM on the second week. Claudia agreed to update and distribute the calendar invites accordingly.  
* **Knowledge Representation and Graph RAG Proposal for CoSAI Risk Map:**   
  * Josiah introduced a proposal for a student collaboration project with Duke University master's students to build a knowledge representation and Graph RAG system for the CoSAI-RM, using OWL and RDF representations. Josiah argued that YAML is insufficiently expressive and that an OWL/RDF graph schema enables better entity disambiguation and precise guidance retrieval via MCP servers.   
  * Jodi and J.R. raised concerns regarding long-term maintenance, hosting requirements, pipeline complexity, and security risks associated with exposing MCP servers. Josiah clarified that the tool can run locally using open-source software without requiring centralized hosting, while Claudia noted that OASIS IT director and their legal counsel need to review data management and security implications.  
* **Testable Risk Map Project Proposal:**  
  * Josiah presented a second student collaboration project with Duke University focused on making CoSAI-RM testable by evaluating whether specific controls actually stop corresponding risks. Josiah explained that the project involves three teams—a harness team adapting software like Atomic Red Team, a red team developing attacks, and a blue team applying real-world controls.   
  * Jodi supported the concept of verifying negative and positive cases for controls but questioned the required resources from CoSAI. Josiah clarified that the project only requires the TSC's blessing to launch under WS2 and does not mandate centralized hosting or infrastructure from the organization.  
* **Process for Launching Workstream Projects and Review Deadlines:**   
  * J.R. and Josiah discussed the administrative process and criteria required for the TSC to approve new workstream projects, with J.R. emphasizing the need to clarify maintenance responsibilities before granting formal approval. J.R. provided feedback that the project architecture documents submitted as ADRs were difficult to read beyond the introduction. Claudia agreed to coordinate with IT director Dustin and Josiah to evaluate the technical and security aspects, aiming to establish a clear path for project launch decisions.  
* **AI Village and RSAC Meeting Agenda:**

  * Claudia mentioned that this potential collaboration between CoSAI and the AI Village  would be added to the PGB agenda on Monday.
