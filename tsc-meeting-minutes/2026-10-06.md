* **Containment Paper and Citation Tool Blog Post:**   
  * J.R. checked on the containment paper deadline, and Ian confirmed that the October 15th target remains on track. Additionally, Claudia reported that Karttik drafted a blog post about the citation tool, which was shared with marketing committee co-chairs for review ahead of their meeting.  
* **TSC Meeting Schedule Change:**  
  * Claudia noted that the next TSC meeting is scheduled for October 13th at 7:00 PM Eastern. David L. suggested updating the TSC GitHub README to highlight meeting times more prominently, which Claudia.  
* **WS2 Proposal for Duke University Collaboration Projects:**  
  *  J.R. introduced a proposal brought forward by Josiah and Vinay to engage Duke University master's students on two new projects: an RDF knowledge graph and a testable risk map, discussed in two previous TSC meetings.  
  * David L. and J.R. debated whether projects like this require enterprise support or if an incubation sandbox model should be established for exploratory work without long-term maintenance commitments. Jason supported trying new collaborative approaches to iterate quickly, even if outputs are experimental.   
  * Josiah emphasized that other WS2 members support exploring these projects and requested TSC buy-in and clear initiation pathways.  
* **Governance Boundaries on External Collaborations:**   
  * David L. asserted that collaborating with external entities requires TSC engagement and approval under governance rules before spinning up meetings or using the project name.  
* **Operational and Legal Review of the Proposal:**   
  * Claudia reported on feedback from OASIS IT Director Dustin regarding operational feasibility, stating there are no blockers for the GraphRAG proposal, though an MCP server cannot be hosted currently and a dedicated maintainer as well as funding would be required if scaled. Claudia added that OASIS legal counsel would need to draft clarifying language regarding tool usage, and an incubator repository could be utilized for the project. Josiah confirmed that all student volunteers signed individual contributor license agreements.  
* **Call for Consensus Process and Timeline:**   
  * Because a quorum was not present, Claudia proposed issuing a call for consensus via email on Wednesday, closing on Friday noon Eastern time to allow Josiah and Vinay to proceed.   
* **Process Friction and Incubation Guidelines Proposal:**   
  * J.R. and David L. addressed project startup delays, acknowledging that open project processes involve friction, consensus requirements, and learning curves. David L. and Jason agreed to collaborate on writing an incubation proposal to define the framework for exploratory donations and projects.  
* **RSAC 2027 Submissions and Alignment:**   
  * Jason presented the RSAC 2027 conference submission spreadsheet with an application deadline of October 9th. J.R., Claudia, and David L. clarified constraints, noting that individuals must submit proposals independently, branded project talks require marketing committee and PGB review.. Jason agreed to follow up with submitters to encourage formal submissions and cross-reference them with workstream leads in parallel.  
* **WS3 Review and Planning:**  
  *  J.R. concluded by noting that WS3 has not yet presented its review, and the co-chairs plan to evaluate workstream organization, lead tenures, licensing, and topical focus moving forward.  
  * Shrey offered to present the WS3 update during either next week's TSC call or the week after. J.R. noted that next week's meeting is scheduled as an evening call, which would accommodate colleagues in Japan.   
* **Leadership Strategy and Incubation Projects:**   
  * J.R. stated that the co-chairs are discussing how to raise the bar and improve work and that they plan to follow up with David and Omar regarding potential changes in direction. These discussions involve previously taken-for-granted areas which may lead to a shift in course.
