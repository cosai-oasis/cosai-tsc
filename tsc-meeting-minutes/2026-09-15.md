* **WS1 Q3 Status and Roadmap:**  
  * Asmae presented the WS1 Q3 status update, highlighting completed deliverables such as the risk and controls paper, model signing paper, and the artifact integrity maturity model, which is moving to publication. * **Workstream 2 Status, Telemetry SIG, and Roadmap Initiatives:**&nbsp;

Roadmap proposals include launching a CoSAI ontology project, developing a testable CoSAI risk-map model in collaboration with master's students from Duke University, and tackling jailbreak characterization led by Jason.&nbsp;  
  * Additionally, Josiah introduced a nomination process for new workstream co-leads, noting that Arthur has self-nominated for the role.  
* **Agenda Reorganization and Issue Management:**  
  * &nbsp;J.R. proposed handling AI-generated action items offline by splitting the meeting agenda into key feature items and a backlog list, allowing participants to review and close issues independently while moving items that require discussion back up into the main agenda. Adding to this proposal, Josiah suggested implementing specific issue tags, such as "work in progress" or "deferred indefinitely," to quickly filter out items that do not require active discussion during the meeting.
