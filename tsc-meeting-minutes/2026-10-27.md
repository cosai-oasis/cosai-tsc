## Meeting Minutes

* **Administrative Updates and WS2 Leadership:**   
  * Claudia announced that Arthur Saputkin has been approved and onboarded as a co-chair for WS2.   
* **Jailbreak Taxonomy and Multimodal SIG Integration:**   
  * J.R. noted an intersection between jailbreak scenarios and the multimodal security SIG in WS4. David expressed concern about scope creep and semantic overlap. Josiah noted he would take the jailbreak proposal document to the multimodal security SIG for feedback, placing the next steps in Jason’s hands to follow up with the multimodal SIG.  
* **Threat Modeling Draft for Security Review Agents:**   
  * Shrey provided an overview of a threat modeling initiative within the AI-assisted code development SIG, focusing on threat modeling a security review agent rather than a code generation agent. Shrey noted a draft exists, and after reviewing it with the group to finalize it, the SIG plans to bring it to the TSC for review.   
* **Incubation Proposal and Project Governance Process:**  
  * David LaBianace and Jason Garman had an action item to draft a proposal for incubating new projects (not workstreams) in CoSAI. Josiah stated he had received initial guidance from OASIS’ legal counsel regarding the two projects with Duke and would follow up with Jason to draft what incubation project proposals should look like. Claudia added that the PGB must have final approval and OASIS staff must review proposals before implementation.  
  * David P. discussed receiving inquiries about external contributions. Claudia stated she owes David a response regarding the process and noted that WS4 has a formal process for suggesting new work via an RFC in the CONTRIBUTING.md document.  
  * Karttik suggested consolidating proposal intake across workstreams Josiah agreed that a consistent, well-understood workflow across workstreams is needed for organizational decisions. Karttik and Josiah noted that the Monday meeting between workstream leads and the TSC leads should serve as the forum for this discussion. Claudia agreed and suggested that finding an alternative time that works for more participants might be necessary.  
* **WS2 Telemetry Paper:**  
  * Josiah provided an update on the WS2 telemetry paper, stating that they require three human reviewers to build consensus. Josiah confirmed the paper review is still pending and in progress.  
* **Documentation Governance and Repository Organization:**   
  * Karttik raised concerns regarding redundant issues related to technical and best practices, suggesting consolidation. He highlighted J.R.'s automated agenda plug-in that pulls and consolidates meeting notes from Google Drive.  
* **WS1 Naming and Scope Discussion:**  
  * Karttik reviewed issue 29 and discussed with Jay whether renaming WS1 from "software supply chain security for AI systems" to "AI supply chain" was a major point of contention. The name change had been suggested by Asmae. Jay stated he did not recall it being a major contention. Jay raised concerns that altering the workstream name from "AI supply chain security" to "AI supply chain" removes focus on critical security protections. Josiah noted that Asmae previously wanted to broaden the scope to include hardware while keeping security within the mandate. Jay questioned whether the original CoSAI charter permits incorporating hardware elements, while David P. suggested that treating hardware like an entitlement could simplify management, though Jay remained cautious about scope creep extending past firmware.  
  * Discussing the broader hardware scope, Claudia stated that the PGB needs to weigh in if the scope is expanded, noting that the topic could also be addressed during the TSC co-chairs' roadmap restructuring or through regular meetings with Omar. Karttik agreed to document this discussion.  
* **Standards Bodies Submission Pathways Status:**   
  * Transitioning to the final agenda item, Karttik inquired whether donation or contribution guidelines were finalized. Claudia clarified that the matter remained open and that they needed to follow up on determining general pathways for submitting work to other standards bodies.
