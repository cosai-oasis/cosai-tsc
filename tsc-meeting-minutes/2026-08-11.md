* **Black Hat and DefCon Debrief:**   
  * Bill provided a summary of the AI Village at DefCon, which included paper presentations, a panel on dual use, a product safety presentation and various contests such as an adversarial capture the flag competition. The event also featured over 30 poster presentations and was described as a successful forum for open, unconstrained technical discussion.  
* **Industry Partnership Opportunities:**   
  * Akila and Karttik discussed the potential to better market CoSAI to new startups and partners. They identified a missed opportunity to recruit and engage these organizations at events like Black Hat, suggesting that creating a dedicated strategy to involve these companies could benefit both the partners and the coalition.  
* **Research Paper Citation Tracking:**  
  * Karttik presented a pull request involving a tool that automatically tracks citations of CoSAI research papers. The tool identifies formal publications and subsequent references, providing a way to validate the adoption and impact of the work produced by the organization.  
  * Klaudia suggested that CoSAI consider using ArXiv for future research publications to improve visibility and citation tracking. Claudia agreed to discuss this internally to determine if there are any restrictions, while Karttik noted that while ArXiv has rigorous vetting processes, it remains a valuable option to explore.  
  * Karttik reported that the tool identified 12 formal publications and 98 citing articles, with 146 total references found. Notably, the paper on MCP Security from WS4 received significant attention.  
  * Daniel argued that while citations are a useful metric, measuring true impact requires evidence of implementation and action, such as instances of model signing. Ian and Daniel agreed that demonstrating cause and effect regarding how documents translate into real-world security practices is the desired goal for the organization.  
  * The group discussed where the citation tracking tool should be hosted. The consensus was that the TSC should own the tool for decision-making purposes, while the marketing committee should utilize the data to demonstrate adoption and value to potential partners.  
* **GitHub Integration for Adoption:**  
  * Josiah proposed that the organization require accompanying code or skills in the GitHub repository for each paper to help organizations operationalize deliveries. J.R. supported this, suggesting that the GitHub-first architecture group lead this effort to monitor the uptake of tools like the CoSAI Risk Map.   
* **Zero Trust Paper Review:**   
  * Akila provided feedback on the Zero Trust paper from WS2, suggesting that the appendix be reorganized for readability and that the team move beyond relying solely on human oversight. She noted that their feedback was not intended as a hard blocker but rather as an opportunity to frame human oversight as a scaling mechanism that should evolve toward autonomous security controls.  
  * Akila recommended that the marketing committee leverage specific, high-impact tables and content from the Zero Trust paper for social media posts, such as on LinkedIn, to garner greater engagement and visibility.  
* **AI Containment and Isolation Policies:**   
  * Daniel, J.R. and Ian discussed the urgent need to address sandbox policy enforcement and containment, noting that current risks are highlighted by recent incidents. The group agreed to discuss this topic further at the next meeting to potentially develop a blog or point of view document on isolation and containment.  
* **AI Maturity Model Paper Review:**   
  * J.R. reviewed the AI Maturity Model paper, praising the layout of maturity levels but noting that the document could be improved by providing more guidance on how to operationalize these levels within an enterprise   
  * Jay and Asmae explained that the initial plan for the paper was to focus on education, with a follow-up document dedicated to reference implementations and examples. The group agreed that the next steps should focus on creating concrete, actionable implementation guides rather than writing additional papers.  
* **End of Term Review for Workstreams and associated SIGs:**  
  *  J.R. concluded the meeting by announcing that his and Akila’s tenure as co-chairs will end at the end of the month and that they plan to review all four workstreams and SIGs in the upcoming TSC meeting.
